Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains what information beatusmidias.com collects, why, and how it is used. Beatus Mídias (legal name Bruno Lucas Marketing LTDA) operates this site as a paid traffic and AI automation agency for restaurants and delivery brands in the United States.

Information you submit

When you fill out our contact form, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, and optionally your WhatsApp number, business name, city, delivery platforms used, and monthly ad budget range. We use this information only to respond to your request for a free audit and to follow up about our services. We do not sell this information to third parties.

How your submission reaches us

Form submissions are sent to our internal systems (an automation platform and our support inbox) so our team can respond within 24 hours. If you choose to submit via WhatsApp or email instead of the form, the same information is shared directly with us through that channel.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site. These tools may set cookies that track pageviews and general usage patterns. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies using the banner shown on your first visit; declining disables analytics and advertising cookies, not the core functioning of the site.

Third-party services

  • Google Tag Manager / Analytics, site usage analytics, subject to Google's own privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp, if you contact us via the WhatsApp button or form fallback, your message is handled per WhatsApp's own privacy terms.
  • Email, messages sent to hello@beatusmidias.com are handled through our email provider.

Data retention and your rights

We keep contact-form submissions only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and, if you become a client, to provide our services. You can request that we delete your information at any time by emailing hello@beatusmidias.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to hello@beatusmidias.com.
