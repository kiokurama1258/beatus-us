Questions home service companies ask us.

What is the best advertising for home service businesses?

For most home service companies the strongest mix is Google Local Services Ads for people searching right now, Google search campaigns for specific high-value jobs, Meta ads for recurring and seasonal services and a complete Google Business Profile with real reviews. What ties them together is a fast reply to every lead and tracking by booked job.

What are Google Local Services Ads?

Local Services Ads are Google ads for service providers that appear at the top of search results. Customers can call or message you directly from the ad, you pay for leads related to your services instead of clicks, and eligible businesses can earn the Google Verified badge after Google's screening. Google also considers how quickly you respond.

Should a contractor use Local Services Ads or Google search ads?

Usually both. Local Services Ads bring calls and messages from people who want a provider now and need little management once set up. Search ads give you control over job types, landing pages and schedules, which matters for high-value work like replacements and installs. We measure both by cost per booked job and move budget accordingly.

How much should a home service company spend on marketing?

Start from capacity and job value. Know how many extra jobs your crews can take each month, what an average job or recurring customer is worth and what you can pay to win one. Set a test budget for the most profitable job types, track every lead to the booked job and scale what pays back.
