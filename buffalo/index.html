Restaurant marketing in Buffalo

The city that put wings on the map.

Buffalo is widely credited as the birthplace of the Buffalo wing. The most commonly told account traces it to the Anchor Bar on Main Street, where on March 4, 1964, Teressa Bellissimo tossed deep-fried chicken wings in hot sauce and served them with a side of blue cheese, a recipe the bar still serves today at its original location, open since 1935. The origin story is disputed: a competing claim from John Young says he was serving wings in Buffalo as early as 1961, and some accounts hold that Anchor Bar didn't add wings to its regular menu until 1974.

That legacy shows up every Labor Day weekend at the National Buffalo Wing Festival, held in Buffalo since 2002. The festival has drawn well over 500,000 visitors over the years, with an eating contest that has crowned champions like Joey Chestnut and Miki Sudo. The market behind it is real too: Buffalo's city population is approximately 278,000, but the Buffalo-Niagara Falls metro area is home to approximately 1.16 million people, plenty of appetite for a restaurant with the right marketing behind it.
