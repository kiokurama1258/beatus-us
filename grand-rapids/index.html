A city that just won "Best Beer City" five years running.

Grand Rapids carries an official title with a real paper trail: "Beer City USA." The name started as a tie with Asheville, North Carolina, in a 2012 online poll created by homebrewing pioneer Charlie Papazian, then Grand Rapids won it outright in 2013 and the poll was retired. Since then, USA Today's Readers' Choice Awards has named Grand Rapids "Best Beer City" for five consecutive years, 2021 through 2025, with the most recent win announced March 5, 2025. The metro area now counts nearly 40 breweries. That is not local pride talking — it's a national outlet handing the city the trophy, year after year.

The demand behind that reputation is measurable. Restaurant Week GR brought together more than 70 local restaurants for its 16th edition in November 2025, and the 2026 edition is already confirmed for November 6 to 14. The city's accommodation and food services sector posted $804,839,000 in sales in the most recent economic census, and a combined student population of roughly 34,000 studies downtown or nearby, split between Grand Rapids Community College (about 12,468 students) and Grand Valley State University (about 22,011 students, with its Pew Campus right in the city). DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub are all active in the market; at the time of writing, Grubhub's own Grand Rapids page listed 761 restaurants and stores available for delivery. The city itself is dense and compact — 201,183 people across 44.78 square miles, up 1.2% since the 2020 census, with a median household income of $69,108. That's a real customer base for any restaurant working to reach it.
