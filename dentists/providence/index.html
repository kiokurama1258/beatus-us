Who regulates dental advertising in Rhode Island?

Dentists in Rhode Island are licensed by the Rhode Island Board of Examiners in Dentistry, part of the Rhode Island Department of Health. State dental boards can regulate how dentists advertise, for example how specialties and claims are described, so every ad we write is reviewed and approved by your practice before it goes live. When in doubt about a specific claim, check the board's current rules.

How much does it cost to hire a dental marketing agency?

There are two separate costs: your ad spend, which you set and which goes straight to Google and Meta, and the agency's management fee. Beatus starts with a free audit to size a budget your schedule can absorb, with no long-term lock-in contract. We measure the result as cost per booked patient, not cost per click.

What does the AI patient assistant actually do?

It replies to every new inquiry by text, chat or messaging app within seconds, day or night. It answers common questions using only information your practice approved, offers appointment times and hands the conversation to your team whenever needed. It never diagnoses or gives clinical advice, and anything urgent goes straight to a person.
