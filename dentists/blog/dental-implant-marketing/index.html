Dental Implant Marketing: From Lead to Booked Consult

By Bruno Vasconcellos, founder of Beatus Mídias
October 1, 2026 · 6 min read
Direct answer: Dental implant marketing is a long sale, not an impulse buy. Patients research, compare and worry about cost before booking a consult. The practices that win run dedicated implant campaigns, explain the process honestly, answer the price question instead of dodging it, reply within minutes and keep following up for weeks with people who are not ready yet.

Why do implant leads behave differently?

An implant is a large, planned decision. Most people want to understand the process, how long it takes, whether they are a candidate and how they can pay. They often ask several practices the same questions. Whoever answers clearly and stays in touch has the best chance of getting the consult.

How should you handle the price question?

Every implant lead asks about price. Refusing to answer sounds evasive, and a single made-up number creates disappointment at the consult. The honest middle ground: explain what changes the cost, such as the number of implants, bone grafting or the type of restoration, share a range if your practice is comfortable with it, and explain that the exact plan comes after the exam. Mention financing only if you offer it.

How do you follow up with people who are not ready?

  1. Answer every question in the first conversation and offer two consult times.
  2. If they don't book, follow up a few days later with useful information, not pressure.
  3. Over the next weeks, share a patient story, an answer to a common fear and a reminder that the consult is the next step.
  4. Stop when they ask you to, and record the reason in your CRM so you learn what blocks the decision.

This is where most implant leads are won or lost. See how to build that routine in our guide to dental lead follow-up.

What should you measure?

Measure cost per implant consult and the share of consults that accept treatment, not just cost per lead. A campaign with cheap leads that never show up costs more than one with fewer leads that book and say yes.

The bottom line

Implant patients reward the practice that is clear, patient and quick. Dedicated campaigns bring the leads. Honest answers and steady follow-up turn them into consults and cases.

Frequently asked questions

Should dental implant prices be shown in ads?

It depends on your market, your state's advertising rules and how standardized your cases are. Any advertised price must be accurate and include the conditions that apply. Many practices prefer to share a range in the conversation and confirm the plan after the exam, which avoids attracting people who only want the lowest number.

How long does it take for an implant lead to book?

It varies a lot. Some people book a consult on the first contact, while others take weeks or months to research, save or talk with family. That is why implant marketing needs a follow-up sequence and a CRM. Without them, the practice pays to generate leads and forgets them before they are ready.

Is a free implant consult a good offer?

It can lower the barrier to the first visit, but it also attracts people who are only collecting quotes. Some practices charge a small fee that is credited toward treatment to filter for serious patients. Test both in your market and compare consults that turn into accepted treatment, not just the number of consults.
