Aligners

Invisalign and Clear Aligner Marketing for Dentists

By Bruno Vasconcellos, founder of Beatus Mídias
October 1, 2026 · 6 min read
Direct answer: Invisalign marketing for dentists works best as a consult-driven campaign: Meta ads to reach adults who have thought about straighter teeth for years, Google search ads for people already comparing providers, a page that explains the process and payment options, and fast follow-up. Aligner patients decide over weeks, so the practice that keeps answering clearly usually gets the case.

Who are aligner patients?

Many are adults who wanted straighter teeth for a long time and postponed it because of cost, time or the idea of metal braces. Others are parents asking for their teenagers. Both groups research a lot, ask about price early and compare providers before booking a consult.

What should the aligner page include?

  • Who is a good candidate and who may need another option.
  • The steps, from consult and scan to the final retainer.
  • Payment and financing options you actually offer.
  • Real cases, only with written consent and within your state's rules.
  • A clear way to book the consult.

How should you use the brand name?

Invisalign is a trademark of Align Technology. If your practice is a provider, follow Align's current brand guidelines when you use the name in ads and on your site. Many practices also use the generic term "clear aligners", which people search for as well.

How do you handle the price question?

Explain what changes the cost, such as case complexity and treatment length, share a range if your practice is comfortable with it and say that the exact plan comes after the scan. Then offer a consult time. A clear answer keeps the conversation going. A dodge ends it.

The bottom line

Aligner patients are planners. Reach them where they postpone the decision, give them honest answers and keep the conversation going until the consult is on the calendar.

Frequently asked questions

Are Facebook ads good for Invisalign?

Often yes, because many aligner patients are not actively searching. They have thought about it for years and need a reason to act now. Meta ads with a real team, clear steps and an easy first conversation can bring those people in, as long as someone replies to every lead within minutes.

Should a dental practice offer a free aligner consult?

It can lower the barrier, but it also attracts people only collecting quotes. Some practices offer a free initial conversation and charge for records, while others credit a small fee toward treatment. Test what fits your market and measure consults that start treatment, not only consults booked.

How long does it take an aligner lead to decide?

It varies widely. Some patients book on the first message, while others take weeks or months to talk with family, check payment options or compare providers. That is why aligner marketing needs a follow-up sequence and a CRM, so leads are not forgotten before they are ready.
