3 More Mid-Size U.S. Cities Ready for Smarter Restaurant Marketing

By Beatus Mídias · Published September 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer: New Haven, Milwaukee and Sacramento each have a beloved, documented James Beard America's Classics institution: a pizzeria that just turned 100, a grille famous for its butter burger, and the restaurant that anchors a city's self-declared "Farm-to-Fork Capital" branding. None of it is marketing spin; it's dated, sourced and current.

New Haven: 100 years of apizza

Frank Pepe Pizzeria Napoletana, founded in New Haven in 1925, was named a James Beard Foundation America's Classics honoree in 1999, one of the award's original cohort. It celebrated its 100th anniversary in June 2025. New Haven is widely recognized in food media as the home of "New Haven-style apizza," a distinct coal-fired pizza tradition with its own national following.

Milwaukee: two classics, one city

Milwaukee has two James Beard Foundation America's Classics winners: Three Brothers Serbian Restaurant, open since 1956 and honored in 2002, and Solly's Grille, open since 1936 and honored in 2022 for its Wisconsin butter burger. Two Beard-recognized institutions from two different eras of Milwaukee's food history, both still serving today, is a real signal of staying power.

Sacramento: the self-declared Farm-to-Fork Capital

Frank Fat's, a Sacramento institution founded in 1939 two blocks from the California State Capitol, won the James Beard Foundation America's Classics award in 2013. Sacramento's mayor also declared the city "America's Farm-to-Fork Capital" back in 2012, a civic branding, not a third-party award, but one that anchors an annual festival local news outlets have estimated draws around 100,000 attendees.

A beloved institution still needs a channel to send new customers to

A James Beard Classics badge or a well-known civic food brand gets a city's food scene talked about. It doesn't automatically get any single restaurant a table on a Tuesday night. That still takes a real paid traffic strategy pointed at your own ordering channel, not a marketplace listing.
